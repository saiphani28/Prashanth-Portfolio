About

I’m Prashanth. A filmmaker, and a storyteller at heart.

I’ve been part of bringing stories from an idea on paper to something people can watch.

From writing and assisting with direction to managing shoots and figuring out how to get things done when plans don’t go as expected, no two experiences have been the same.

And yes, sometimes two days of shooting have to happen in one!

What I enjoy most about filmmaking is observing people, their conversations, emotions and everyday experiences, and finding stories in them.

I want to turn those little observations into characters and stories that feel real, relatable and worth watching.

“Manchi cinema chusam!”

That’s the kind of feeling I hope my films leave behind.

Featured work · 2026

Evare Nuvvu!

Telugu romantic comedy-drama · 6 episodes
Written & directed by Prashanth

What happens when someone knows your story before you’ve even met them?

Gautham is an IT professional who has his own way of looking at life, while Manasa is a cheerful Zumba trainer who seems to bring energy wherever she goes. When their paths cross, things aren’t quite as simple as they appear. Manasa knows more about Gautham than he could imagine, and there’s a reason behind the distance she maintains.

As their stories unfold, hidden connections, unexpected turns and complicated relationships bring questions of trust, respect, consent and loyalty to the surface.

A light-hearted romantic comedy with an emotional side, Evare Nuvvu! explores how much we really know about the people we meet, and how the past can shape the relationships we build.
